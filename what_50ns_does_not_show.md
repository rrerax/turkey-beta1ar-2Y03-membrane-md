# What this sampling does not show

The calculation starts from isoprenaline in the 2Y03 crystal, chain A, in a POPC membrane. The three replicates change only the velocity seed. Three things were specified beforehand: the amine hydrogen to Asp121, phenolic hydrogen OAB to Ser211, phenolic hydrogen OAC to Ser215, and water between each of those three pairs. Water cannot turn a hydrogen bond that did not form into a contact that is still present. Ser212 and the benzylic oxygen are recorded only.

Production length is set by the speed of this card, lands on 10, 20, or 50 ns, and is written to `run_plan.json` before contacts are counted. The measured 5,000 steps took 25.0 seconds, about 69 ns/day, so each production run is 50 ns. Once the production runs were going, the rate was about 72 ns/day, and one 50 ns run took about 17 hours. The three replicates ran one after another on this one card. Equilibration frames do not enter the occupancy. A contact persists when its occupancy in the production frames is at least 0.20 and at least two of the three replicates meet that bar.

This length cannot show affinity. Ligand exit from a β-receptor pocket is a slower process. Staying for a few tens of nanoseconds cannot be converted into a binding constant and cannot be compared with an experimental activity. An RMSD that reaches a plateau only says that the trajectory stopped drifting further. It does not say how strong the binding is. One trajectory that keeps a contact is not the result. The question is whether the three replicates do the same thing.

It also cannot speak for CHEMBL776, for a docking pose on 3SN6, or for the manually placed frame. Those coordinates are not the starting point. It cannot give a free-energy difference, it cannot say what a mutation would do, and it cannot explain why a docking score gives a worse number to a pose whose geometry is otherwise right. The force field, the protonation, and this stabilised turkey β1 construct are all still inside the result. Human and turkey, and β1 and β2, are not the same receptor.

It can show only this: under the force field that was written down, and during this production time, whether the three 2Y03 crystal hydrogen bonds and the water near them are still occupied under three independent velocities. `analyze_contacts.py` writes the contact figure after all three production runs, in `contacts_three_replicates.png` and `contact_result.json`.

Each production run is 50 ns and 5,000 frames. At the end of equilibration the CA RMSD of the transmembrane core from the start is 0.105, 0.103, and 0.114 nm, all below 0.30 nm. Production occupancies of the three hydrogen bonds are: amine–Asp121, 0.346, 0.327, 0.607; OAB–Ser211, 0.233, 0.243, 0.004; OAC–Ser215, 0.227, 0.327, 0.266. Under the rule “at least 0.20, and in at least two of the three replicates,” all three hydrogen bonds persist. OAB–Ser211 is almost absent in the third replicate. All three water occupancies are below 0.06, so the waters are not present. The median heavy-atom RMSD of the ligand against the transmembrane core is 0.91, 0.93, and 1.34 Å. That RMSD is not an affinity. Trajectories, checkpoints, and the solvated system are not in this repository.
